Koroai 頃合い

Find a pace that feels right, and choose your next step with confidence.

Overview

Koroai helps you see how long it has been since an everyday activity, how often you have done it recently, and how that compares with a guideline you set for yourself.

It is not about judging success or failure. Koroai gives you a calm, useful reference so you can decide whether now feels like the right time.

Feature Highlights

Know when the time feels right

See the days since your last activity or your recent count at a glance, alongside the pace you chose.

Record in a few taps

Add today's activity quickly, or record an earlier date when you remember it later.

Look back calmly

Use a monthly calendar to revisit your history, notes, and related photos without scores or streak pressure.

Set your own pace

Create activities in your own words and choose a guideline for building a habit, adjusting frequency, or simply keeping a record.
